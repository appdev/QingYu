WHY QINGYU

In an age of bloated tools, a simple notebook

AI can look up facts in seconds. What's worth writing down are your own reflections and private thoughts. Most note apps keep piling on plugins, collaboration and clutter, while lighter ones miss cross-platform apps or sync. QingYu brings both together: as simple as a paper notebook, ready on every device.

As light as Notes

No plugin store, no layout fiddling—just open and write. Headings, tables and checklists render in place, with syntax out of the way.

Cross-platform & encrypted

The two things light note apps usually miss. Notes are encrypted on your device before upload so the cloud only sees ciphertext, and conflicts keep both copies.

Notes are just files

Never locked in a private database. Folders are directories and every note is a plain .md file on your disk—still yours even if you uninstall QingYu.

WRITING

Every format you need, no syntax to learn

A familiar three-column layout: folders, a note list grouped by Today, Yesterday and Previous 7 Days, and the editor. If you've used Apple Notes, you already know it.

  • Edit table cells directly, tick off to-dos with a click
  • Footnotes, math and callouts whenever you need them
  • Diagrams and mind maps right inside your notes (Mermaid, Markmap)
  • Type / for the slash menu to insert anything
  • Fold headings, jump to any note with ⌘K, search full text

JOTS

Write it down before it slips away

No title, no folder to pick. Jots line up by day in one timeline, like texting yourself: a passing thought, a line from a book, something to do.

  • Group with #tags, filter by tag or to-dos, or search
  • Attach photos, or record a voice note
  • Pin what matters, and turn a jot into a full note when it grows
  • On your phone, long-press the app icon to start a jot or a voice note
  • Every jot is still a plain .md file, right alongside your notes

MOBILE

Rebuilt for the way you hold a phone

Not a shrunken desktop. Large titles, rounded groups, and search and new-note buttons at the bottom, right where your thumb is.

  • A floating format bar for text styles, to-dos, tables and attachments
  • Follows system light and dark; dark mode is true black for late nights
  • The same interface on Android and iPhone (Android now, iOS not yet)

SHARING

Turn a note into an image

Pick a backdrop (hills, seaside, dusk, orchard or a solid color), preview it, then copy or save. Ready for any chat or social feed.

  • For long notes, select a passage and share just that part
  • Or send the text through the system share sheet, or copy it as plain text

APPEARANCE

Default, Paper, Sepia, Slate, Forest and High Contrast, each in light and dark, following your system if you like. Body font and size are adjustable too.

SYNC & PRIVACY

Fully usable without an account

Your notes live only in the local folder you choose. When you need more than one device, pick a way to sync.

Effortless

QingYu Cloud

Sign in with a QingYu account and turn it on. It syncs when you open the app and save a note, and every 3 minutes after that. Manage your account and storage in the account center.

Your own

Custom S3

Bring any S3-compatible storage: AWS, Cloudflare R2, MinIO and more. Where the data lives and for how long is entirely up to you.

Write on your device→ Encrypt with your sync password (AES-256-GCM)→ Cloud stores ciphertext only→ Decrypt on another device

Notes, images and attachments are all encrypted. If you forget the sync password and have no unlocked device left, the cloud copy cannot be decrypted, so keep it safe.

Prefer not to use cloud sync? You can simply put your notes folder in iCloud Drive or Dropbox.

FAQ

Good questions

Is QingYu free?

Yes. The app is free and open source under GPL-3.0, and every writing feature works without an account. QingYu Cloud is an optional sync service; syncing through your own S3 or a cloud drive costs nothing.

Where are my notes stored?

In the local folder you choose. Folders are directories on disk and each note is a plain .md file, with images and attachments next to it. Uninstall QingYu and the files stay exactly where they are.

What if I forget my sync password?

The sync password stays on your devices and the server cannot recover it. If another device is still unlocked, you can reset it there. Otherwise the encrypted cloud copy cannot be opened, though the local note files on each device are unaffected.

macOS says the app can't be opened?

The macOS build is not notarized yet. If it's blocked on first launch, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". The Windows installer is unsigned and may trigger SmartScreen; choose "More info → Run anyway".

Which Markdown syntax is supported?

GitHub-flavored tables, task lists and strikethrough, plus footnotes, KaTeX math, callouts, Mermaid diagrams and Markmap mind maps. Your files still render properly in other Markdown tools.

Is there an iPhone or iPad app?

The interface is ready, but the iOS app isn't on the App Store yet. Downloads will appear here once it is. On Android you can install the APK directly.

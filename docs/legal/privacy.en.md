# QingYu Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 2026-08-13

## 1. Developer and scope

QingYu is maintained by the individual developer “QingYu developer appdev.” This Policy applies to software functions directly provided by QingYu and this repository. It does not govern third-party plugins, themes, synchronization targets, storage providers, websites, or external links.

Developer: https://github.com/appdev; project: https://github.com/appdev/QingYu; website: https://apkdv.com/; email: lengyue@apkdv.com.

## 2. Local data and processing

QingYu is centered on the local workspace you select. Documents, attachments, settings, indexes, history, caches, and logs are generally processed and stored on your device and in that workspace. You are responsible for its location, device access controls, backups, encryption, and recovery.

The current code review did not identify a developer-operated backend for usage analytics, advertising tracking, or installation-event collection. This is not an absolute statement that the software never connects to a network: features, content, and extensions that you enable may do so.

## 3. Optional network features

When you enable synchronization, WebDAV, S3, publishing, MCP, remote images or resources, a proxy, third-party plugins, or other external capabilities, relevant documents, attachments, requests, credential-derived information, and device or network information may be sent to the services you select to fulfill your request. The provider and your configuration determine the data, purpose, retention, and processing location.

appdev currently operates no QingYu account, membership, or official cloud-sync backend. Application version updates do not connect to SiYuan version or package services. Networking by plugins, themes, or other extensions is separate from application version updates.

## 4. Logs, diagnostics, and feedback

QingYu may locally record errors, performance information, file paths, system environment details, and operational context for troubleshooting. You decide whether to disclose these materials by exporting logs, sending email, or submitting feedback. Before sending, remove passwords, keys, private document content, and anything unnecessary.

## 5. Third-party extensions and services

Plugins, themes, embedded pages, external links, and storage or synchronization services that you configure are controlled by third parties. They may have independent permissions and privacy practices. The QingYu developer cannot control their security, availability, or data handling. Review their origin, permissions, and policies before use.

## 6. Retention, deletion, and portability

You generally decide how long local data is retained. You may delete, export, or migrate it through the software or file system, but deleting a local copy does not automatically delete backups, history snapshots, synchronization targets, or third-party copies. Use each relevant provider's mechanisms as well.

## 7. Security

QingYu uses measures such as open-source review, access controls, optional encryption, and transport security to reduce risk, but no software, device, or network is absolutely secure. Use a supported operating system, protect devices and credentials, verify backups, and install extensions cautiously.

## 8. Children's privacy

QingYu is not an online service designed specifically for children, and the developer does not intentionally create profiles of children's personal information. Minors should use the software and any external service with guardian consent and supervision. Contact the developer if a child's personal information was mistakenly sent in feedback.

## 9. International processing and legal rights

Local use generally does not require sending data to the developer. Services you select or email you send may be processed in another country or region. Applicable law may provide mandatory rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, or complaint; this Policy does not exclude them.

## 10. Changes and contact

The developer will update this Policy and its date in the project repository when features or data practices materially change. Material changes should receive reasonable notice before taking effect. Contact lengyue@apkdv.com with privacy questions.

This Policy is available in multiple languages. The Simplified Chinese version is the reference in case of an interpretive difference, without excluding mandatory rights under applicable law. This code-based transparency statement is not legal advice.
